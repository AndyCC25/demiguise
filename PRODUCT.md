# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js with `output: 'export'` (fully static), deployed to GitHub Pages. Base path must be configured in `next.config` to match the repo name. No server-side runtime; all pages pre-rendered at build time.

## Users

Potential customers and consumers evaluating Aurium's past work before reaching out. They arrive to see what kinds of custom melamine pieces have been made, judge quality and style fit, and decide whether to commission work. Browsing is the primary job — no purchase or booking flow on-site.

## Product Purpose

Aurium is a custom melamine furniture and design workshop. This site displays a curated catalog of completed projects — "Trabajos en melamina con diseños personalizados" — so prospective clients can assess craftsmanship and envision what Aurium could make for them. Success is a visitor who leaves confident enough to make contact.

## Positioning

A portfolio that shows finished, real work — not renders, not stock — made to order for specific clients. Personalization and tangible craft are the proof. A generic furniture retailer cannot truthfully show the same thing.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse on mobile or desktop, likely arriving via social media or word of mouth. The catalog is the primary content; no account, cart, or checkout exists. Images are the product. The business operates in a Spanish-language market.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- ~130 real product photos available in `images/` (WhatsApp-exported JPEGs).
- Static site only: no CMS, no server, no dynamic routes at runtime.
- GitHub Pages deployment constrains the base URL to the repository path.
- Primary language: Spanish. No multilingual requirement established.
- No e-commerce, inquiry form, or booking flow confirmed for this version.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** Aurium
- **Logo:** Geometric arch/doorway mark in deep forest green with rounded charcoal wordmark (confirmed asset in `images/msedge_6Fp9NfhDzU.png`).
- **Color identity:** Deep forest green + off-white + dark charcoal — committed by the logo; future work must extend, not override, this palette.
- **Tagline (working):** "Trabajos en melamina con diseños personalizados"

## Evidence on Hand

- `images/` — ~130 JPEG product photographs, real completed projects, ready to use.
- `images/msedge_6Fp9NfhDzU.png` — Aurium logotype (green arch mark + wordmark).
- No written testimonials, pricing, or client names on hand; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. **The work speaks.** Every surface decision defers to the photographs. Copy and chrome exist only to frame them.
2. **Mobile first, always.** Target visitors arrive from social links on phones; desktop is an enhancement.
3. **Craft legibility over decoration.** Layout must let material texture and joinery read clearly — no visual noise competing with the photos.
4. **Spanish-first.** All copy, labels, and headings default to Spanish unless the brief explicitly adds a second language.
5. **Static simplicity.** No feature that requires a server. Reliability and fast load on modest connections outrank interactivity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific accessibility standard established. Default to WCAG 2.1 AA as the working floor.
